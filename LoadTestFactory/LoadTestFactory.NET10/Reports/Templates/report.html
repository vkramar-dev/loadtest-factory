How to read this report

All counts, RPS and percentiles refer to the configured measurement window. Average RPS is divided by the configured duration, including quiet seconds. Failed operations count toward latency and throughput. The throughput chart uses per-second completion counts. The latency chart displays per-second mean and maximum, not per-second P95/P99. Longer test timelines are grouped to at most 300 points; latency means are weighted by completed operation count.
